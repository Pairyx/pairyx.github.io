How it works

Today Pairyx finds the fit and closes the deal. The platform we’re building keeps everything after that in the same place, from the affiliate links to the legal paperwork.

  1. You tell us what you need

    Brands share a brief: category, target audience, budget, values and the deal on the table. Creators share their audience, niche, content style, engagement and the deal structures they accept.

  2. We score every pairing

    Four things decide a match: how much the audiences overlap, whether the categories line up, whether the economics are feasible, and whether the creator’s voice and values fit the brand. A creator far over budget, or one who only takes a deal type you aren’t offering, is flagged before they’re ever recommended.

  3. You get a ranked shortlist

    Pairyx reads each brief and bio for meaning as well as keywords, so it finds creators whose tone and values fit even when their category label doesn’t. Every finalist comes with its reasons in plain language a founder can act on.

  4. We pitch, negotiate and close

    We reach out on your behalf, negotiate the terms and deliver a signed contract.

How a deal can be paid

Cash
A flat fee paid on delivery.
Cash and equity
Part fee, part ownership, so the creator shares in the brand’s long-term growth.
Cash and revenue share
A smaller base fee plus a cut of attributable sales.
Revenue share only
No fee up front. The creator is paid on what the campaign sells.

Every deal improves the next match

As deals run through Pairyx, we record what actually happened: engagement, conversions and return on spend.

Over time the model learns which partnerships succeed, and the shortlists get sharper with each deal.
