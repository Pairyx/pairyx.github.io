A look inside the platform.

Pairyx is building one place for brands, creators and our team to run a deal once the match is made. These are real screens from the portal, recorded with demo data. It’s in testing and not open to clients yet.

Set up the deal

In development

Choose the brand and the creator, then set each side’s rate, the budget and the term. Those terms drive everything that follows: the documents, the tracking and the commission.

Prepare and sign the paperwork

In development

Each agreement is filled in from the deal’s terms, sent for signature in order and recorded once both sides have signed. The checklist each side follows updates itself.

Track every sale

In development

Every partnership gets its own link and discount code. Each sale through them shows up with what it earns the creator and Pairyx, as the brand sees it.

Invoices and payment

In development

Pairyx’s invoices sit next to the deal they come from, and a brand can pay one by bank debit from the same screen.

Demo data: the brands, creators, sales and amounts in these clips are made up.
